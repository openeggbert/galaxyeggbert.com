Version 0.4.1 of the 3D remake of Speedy Blupi, running right here in your browser.

  • The game downloads about 100 MB when it starts.
  • Your progress, demos and worlds are saved in this browser.
  • A keyboard or gamepad is best. Touch screens get on-screen controls.
  • Press F1 in the game for help, or Esc to pause.

Your browser doesn't seem to support WebGL 2, which the game needs. Try a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox with hardware acceleration turned on, or download the game.

You're on a phone or tablet. The web version is experimental there and needs a recent device with plenty of memory.
